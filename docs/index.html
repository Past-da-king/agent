Its own browser

Ask it to find something. It goes and looks.

It has a browser of its own that keeps working while you use other apps. Helpers split the job up, and anything that costs money waits for your yes.

Memory

Tell it about your mom once. It remembers.

Everyone and everything it learns about gets a page in a wiki on your phone, linked to the rest. You can read and change every word.

Routines

It can brief you every morning or keep an eye on a price for weeks. It runs on a clock or when a notification comes in, and only bothers you when something actually changed.
